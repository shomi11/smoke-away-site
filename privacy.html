Smoke Away
by Byte Work

Privacy policy

Effective November 22, 2026

Smoke Away is a quit-smoking app for iPhone and Apple Watch made by Byte Work (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is kept, and the choices you have.

  • We don’t collect what you enter. There are no accounts, advertising or tracking.
  • App Store purchases, app opens and a few app events (such as when the Pro screen was shown) are reported to RevenueCat under a random, anonymous ID, so we can see sales statistics.
  • What you enter stays on your devices and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage. We can’t see it.
  • Apple Health data is only read and shown to you. It is never stored by the app, written back, or sent anywhere.
  • Personal notes are written on your device by Apple Intelligence. What you log is never sent to us, to Apple or to any AI service.
  • You can delete everything at any time in Settings › Delete all data.

Information you enter

To track your progress, the app keeps the information you give it:

  • your name or nickname;
  • your answers to the questions when you first open the app: your age range, why you want to quit, what makes you reach for a cigarette, the hardest time of day, how long you’ve smoked, how soon after waking you have your first cigarette, earlier attempts to quit, how ready you feel and how you want to quit;
  • how many cigarettes you smoked per day, the price of a pack and cigarettes per pack;
  • your quit date (which can be a day you plan ahead), the time of your last cigarette and previous quit attempts;
  • cigarettes you log each day;
  • cravings you log, with their trigger, intensity, whether you smoked, and an optional note;
  • your savings goal and cut-down plan, if you set them;
  • Ember’s progress: your evening check-ins, the daily goals you tick off and the days a slip shield covered;
  • your settings, such as reminder times.

The app uses these answers only to build your plan and to tailor the app to you, for example your reasons during a craving and reminders timed for your hardest part of the day. Your age range is never used for anything else, and none of it is used for advertising.

This information is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on for Smoke Away, Apple syncs it to your other devices through your private iCloud database. That database belongs to your Apple Account; we have no access to it. iCloud is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Smoke Away reads your heart rate, heart rate variability and blood oxygen from Apple Health to show how they change after you quit. The app:

  • only reads these values while you look at them and does not save copies;
  • never writes any data to Apple Health;
  • never shares Health data with us or anyone else, and never uses it for advertising or marketing.

You can change this permission at any time in the Health app or in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

Personal notes (Apple Intelligence)

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Smoke Away can write short personal notes during a craving and after a slip, and, with Pro, a reflection on your cravings. They are written by Apple’s on-device language model, which runs entirely on your iPhone. To write a note, the app gives the model only what it needs: your name, your reasons, your usual triggers, your progress and, for a reflection, a summary of your logged cravings and up to three of your recent craving notes.

  • Nothing is sent to us, to Apple or to any other AI service, and it works without an internet connection.
  • The last note of each kind is kept on your device so it doesn’t need to be written again. Delete all data removes it.
  • You can turn notes off at any time in Settings › Personal notes.

Notes are encouragement written by a computer. They can be wrong, and they are not medical advice.

Apple Watch, widgets and Live Activities

The Apple Watch app gets a summary of your progress directly from your iPhone, and cigarettes you log on the watch are sent back to your iPhone. Widgets, controls and Live Activities read the same data on your device. None of this goes through our servers; we don’t have any.

Notifications

Reminders, tips, milestone alerts, a countdown to a planned quit day and a reminder the day before a free trial ends are all scheduled on your device. We don’t send push notifications and don’t receive any information about them.

Purchases

Smoke Away Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple Account information. The app checks with Apple whether Pro is active on your account. Apple’s handling of purchases is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Purchase statistics (RevenueCat)

Starting with version 2.5, Smoke Away uses RevenueCat, a purchase analytics service, so we can see how many people use the app and buy Smoke Away Pro. RevenueCat doesn’t sell anything in the app and doesn’t decide whether you have Pro; Apple and your device do that.

When you open the app, the RevenueCat software creates a random, anonymous app user ID. It sends RevenueCat that ID, your App Store purchase history for Smoke Away (which products you bought, when, at what price and in which App Store country), and basic technical details such as the app version, iOS version, device type and language. Your IP address is visible to RevenueCat’s servers when the app connects. Apple may also notify RevenueCat directly about renewals, cancellations and refunds of Smoke Away Pro.

Nothing you enter in the app, such as your cigarettes, cravings, notes, reasons or Health data, is ever sent to RevenueCat. The purchase information isn’t linked to your name, email address or Apple Account, is used only for statistics, and is never used for advertising or tracking. RevenueCat handles it as described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

Starting with version 3.1, the app also sends a few app events under the same anonymous ID, so we can see where people stop when they first set up the app: the furthest setup step reached (only the step’s name, never your answers), how many times and from where the Smoke Away Pro screen was shown, and whether you chose the free trial, the lifetime purchase or to continue with logging only.

Sharing milestones

When you share a milestone card, the image is created on your device and sent only where you choose in the share sheet. If you choose Save Image, the app asks for permission to add it to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

Contacting us

If you email us or message us on social media, we receive your message and contact details and use them only to reply. We don’t add you to any list. You can ask us to delete our correspondence at any time.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t run servers for the app and don’t collect crash reports or usage statistics beyond the anonymous purchase statistics described above. If you opt in to sharing analytics with developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and usage reports.
  • Apart from RevenueCat for purchase statistics and the app events described above, we don’t use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.
  • We don’t sell or share personal information, and we don’t track you across other apps or websites.

Your choices and rights

Because your information stays on your devices and in your iCloud account, you control it directly:

  • See and change it: everything you entered is shown in the app and can be edited there.
  • Delete it: Settings › Delete all data removes it from this device and from iCloud. Deleting the app removes the data on that device.
  • Stop syncing: turn off iCloud for Smoke Away in the Settings app under your name › iCloud.
  • Purchase records: the records at RevenueCat are anonymous, and we can’t tell which of them is yours. If you’d still like them deleted, email us the date of your purchase and we’ll ask RevenueCat to delete the matching records. Deleting the app removes its anonymous RevenueCat ID.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK or privacy laws in California and other US states), you have rights to access, correct, delete and port your personal information, and to object to its processing. As we hold no personal information about you apart from any messages you send us and the anonymous purchase records described above, you can use these rights in the app as described above, or by emailing us. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Smoke Away is meant for adults who want to stop smoking. It is not directed to children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Health information disclaimer

Smoke Away offers general information and motivation. It is not medical advice and doesn’t replace a doctor or a stop-smoking service.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update it on this page and change the date at the top. If a change affects how your information is handled, we’ll also mention it in the app’s release notes.
